# NaviParka current-product alignment

Updated from the current app screenshots supplied on 17 August 2026.

## Current release messaging
- AI scanner for parking signs and road markings.
- No manual sign/road-marking scan toggle.
- Plain-English interpretation of visible UK parking restrictions.
- Payment provider + location code + direct handoff to an external payment service where available.
- Parking timer and move-car reminders.
- Scan history.
- Vehicle profile/context.
- Blue Badge mode.
- Privacy, sign-out and account deletion controls.

## Roadmap messaging
- Live parking map / nearby parking discovery is NOT presented as a current feature.
- Map is labelled as a later-version / V2 roadmap feature.
- No directions claims in the current product messaging.
